# WorkOrders

Work Orders are at the heart of **Samurai CMMS**. They document every maintenance job that needs to be completed, whether it’s a routine inspection, a repair, or a one-off service request. Each work order helps you plan, assign, track, and record maintenance activities in a consistent and auditable way.

A work order typically includes details such as:

- **Task information:** what needs to be done and any specific instructions  
- **Asset and location:** which equipment or area the job applies to  
- **Priority and due date:** to help schedule and manage workloads  
- **Assigned personnel:** the technician or team responsible for completing the task  
- **Parts and materials:** what resources are required for the job  
- **Completion notes:** what was done, time taken, and any follow-up actions needed  

Work Orders can be created manually, generated automatically from **recurring maintenance schedules**, or raised through **service requests** submitted by staff.

Once active, each work order moves through its lifecycle — **open**, **in progress**, and **completed** — giving you visibility over every stage of your maintenance process.

By managing work orders in Samurai, your organisation gains:

- Better control over maintenance planning and execution  
- Clear visibility of work history and costs  
- Consistent record keeping for compliance and audits
